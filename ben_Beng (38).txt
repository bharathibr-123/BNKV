eti auror borealis dekhaar jany ekati bhaal suyog pradaan kare, yehetu samayer saathe aakaash alp-veshi andhakaar haye thaakave.
yehetu anchalaguli khuv kam janavahul evan haalakaa dooshaner phale praayashai konao samasyaa naa haoyaay, aapani nakshatragulike dekhate paaraven.
jaapaanider kaajer sanskruti yetite pashchimaaraa hayato abhyast taar cheye vesh shrenivaddh evan aanushthaanik.
syutagulo styaandaard vyavasaayer poshaak evan yekhaane sahakarmeeraa eke aparake taader paarivaarik naam vaa kaajer naam dhare daake.
