ehi pathaar sabh ken besee kaal "vidde" ker naam san sandarbhit kael jaayat achhi jekar arth achhi vistrut, khujal aa binaa gaachhak aseem vistaar.
rogalaind aa egadar me ekaraa sabh ken aam roop san "hei" kahal jaaet achhi jekar athe hoyat achhi ekataa binaa gaachh valaa daladal bhoomi je besee kaal heedar san jhaampal rahait achhi.
himashail sabh sthaaee nahi achhi mudaa o parvat san neechaa bahait rahai achhi.. ee phaat, daraar utpann karat je barphak pul san aspasht bhae sakait achhi.
barphak guphaa ker deevar aa chhat dhahi sakait achhi aa phaat band bhae sakait achhi.
himashailak kor par vishaal khand sabh dheel bhae jaayat achhi aa ee neechaa gir jaayat achhi vaa kaat san aagoo taraph garaki jaaet achhi.
hil steshan sabh lel paryatan ker mausam aam roop san bhaarateey garamee mausam me charam par hoit achhi.
on thand mausam me otae ekataa alag sudanrataa aa aakarshan hoit achhi, kaek hil steshan sabh me neek maatraa me barph rahait achhi jatae skeeing aa snobording kae sakait chhee.
keval kichhu eyaralaains ekhanahun shok kiraayaa pesh karait achhi je antim sanskaar lel aakhiree kshanak yaatraa me kichhu chhoot dait achhi.
je eyaralaains sabh ekar peshakash karait achhi ohi me eyar kanaadaa, deltaa eyar laains, yoo.es. vaa kanaadaa se aarambh hoe valaa uraan lel luphthaansaa aa vestajet sammilit achhi.
sabh maamilaa me ahaan eyaralaain ken nishchit roop san sojhe phon kae tikat buk karoo.
