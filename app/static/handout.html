The Big Hack 2.0 · AI Research · R3 The Token Tax

Same question, same answer. Why should a Kannada speaker pay 8.9× more and get a fraction of the AI's memory?

The problem

AI models read and bill tokens, not words. Their tokenizers were built mostly for English, so South Asian languages are cut into many more tokens: up to 13.7× English on identical FLORES-200 sentences. Users pay more, wait longer, and the AI forgets their conversation sooner.

Our solution

BhashaToken rewrites each message into its cheapest form for each AI model (native script, English letters, word-by-word mix, grammar-safe shorter…) and sends it only if a meaning checker proves that numbers, dates, negation, questions, names and every word survived. Every decision is logged for audit.

How it is used

Free, open-source API in front of any AI. An app changes one line (base_url) and every Indian-language message becomes cheaper and meaning-checked. Runs on a CPU laptop (≈35 ms per sentence for 7 tokenizers), no paid API, nothing leaves the computer by default.

Honest limits: costs are simulations; the checker is rule-based and was tested on known error types; answer quality per format is demonstrated live with a small local model, not benchmarked; human ratings are being collected at the event. Every number is reproducible with one command (seeds, data and tokenizer hashes in the app's Evidence page).
